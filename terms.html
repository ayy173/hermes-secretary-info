Terms of use

Hermes Secretary is the operator’s personal assistant configuration. No public account registration or hosted assistant service is offered here.

Authorized use

Use is limited to the operator’s own accounts and information the operator is authorized to process. Connected Google, Telegram, AI and hosting services remain subject to their respective terms and permissions.

Review and approval

AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. The operator should review summaries, drafts and proposed actions, particularly before sending messages, making commitments or changing events. Assistant instructions require specific approval for email sends and calendar changes; these instructions are not a separate technical restriction on the OAuth permissions.

Availability

The assistant depends on the operator’s computer, Hermes, the selected model and connected services. No uptime, uninterrupted connection or error-free output is promised. Account access can expire or be revoked by the operator or Google.

Privacy and independence

The privacy notice describes this configuration’s data practices. This personal configuration is not endorsed by Google or Telegram and is distinct from the Hermes project itself.
